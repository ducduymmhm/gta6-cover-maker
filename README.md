**Heatwave Cover Studio** is an offline web app that turns your own photos and a caption into open-world crime-saga movie key art: a cast grid of tilted panels (3 to 8, with one big hero panel; the default Ensemble layout has 8) where every panel can show a different person, numbered role tags (THE DRIVER, THE MECHANIC, ...), a review banner with a quote and stars, a stacked title with chrome-over-sunset striped letters, extrusion, hard drop shadow and glow, a neon script tagline, and a billing block with condensed credits, a release line, a seal and a barcode strip, all editable or toggleable. Colour grades (Sunset, Neon Night, Miami Dawn, Golden Hour, Mono Noir) add light leaks, halftone dots, bloom and film grain. Load several photos at once and they are dealt one per panel (repeating, as different crops, only when there are more panels than photos); click a panel to swap its photo or edit its tag, drag to pan and scroll to zoom, then export a full-resolution PNG at 1920×1080, 1080×1350 or 1080×1080. Photos never leave your machine. The lettering uses the OFL fonts Anton and Pacifico (`app/fonts/OFL.txt`); the seal, barcode and title treatment are the app's own drawing: no game logos, artwork or fonts.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
