**Heatwave Cover Studio** is an offline web app that turns your own photos and a caption into open-world crime-saga poster art: a grid of tilted panels (3, 4, 5 or 6, with one big hero panel) separated by thin dark gutters, a gradient-map colour grade (Sunset, Neon Night, Miami Dawn, Golden Hour, Mono Noir) with light leaks, halftone dots, bloom and film grain, and big condensed title lettering with an outline, an extruded shadow and a neon script tagline. Drop in one photo or several, click a panel to pick its photo, drag to pan and scroll to zoom, then export a full-resolution PNG at 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080. Photos never leave your machine. The lettering uses the OFL fonts Anton and Pacifico (`app/fonts/OFL.txt`), and the badge and title treatment are original: no game logos, artwork or fonts.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
