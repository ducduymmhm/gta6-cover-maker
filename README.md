**Heatwave Cover Studio** is an offline web app that turns your own photos and a caption into open-world crime-saga movie key art: a cast grid of tilted panels (3 to 9). The default **Cast 9** layout puts the first photo big in the centre as the hero, flanked by two co-stars and six close-ups (in landscape: stacked columns at both edges; in portrait and square: a row across the top and a co-star and close-up each side), with the title over the hero's lower third so no face is ever covered. Any panel can be promoted to the centre with **★ Make hero**. Every panel can show a different person, numbered role tags (THE DRIVER, THE MECHANIC, ...), a review banner with a quote and stars, a stacked title with chrome-over-sunset striped letters, extrusion, hard drop shadow and glow, a neon script tagline, and a billing block with condensed credits, a release line, a seal and a barcode strip, all editable or toggleable. Each photo is auto-levelled on its own before a shared gradient-map grade (Sunset, Neon Night, Miami Dawn, Golden Hour, Mono Noir), so very different photos read as one poster; light leaks, a hero glow, drop shadows, halftone dots, bloom, vignette and film grain finish it. Load several photos at once and they are dealt one per panel in order, the first to the hero (repeating, as different crops, only when there are more panels than photos); click a panel to swap its photo or edit its tag, drag to pan and scroll to zoom, then export a full-resolution PNG at 1920×1080, 1080×1350 or 1080×1080. Photos never leave your machine. The lettering uses the OFL fonts Anton and Pacifico (`app/fonts/OFL.txt`); the seal, barcode and title treatment are the app's own drawing: no game logos, artwork or fonts.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
